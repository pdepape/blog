# Earned-distribution outreach plan

Use this only when an article genuinely references a person, company, or project. Personalize the evidence line; do not send bulk requests.

## Priority targets

1. Authors and product teams cited in the latest weekly roundup.
2. Technical-writing and documentation-operations community organizers.
3. Documentation platform teams whose public work is analyzed in a guide or roundup.
4. Developer-experience practitioners with an audience relevant to the specific article.

## Source-mentioned template

**Subject:** Included your work in this week’s documentation roundup

Hi [name],

I included [specific work] in this week’s TechDocs Studio roundup because [one concrete reason it matters to documentation teams].

Here is the relevant section: [direct article URL]

No action needed—I wanted to share the finished piece and make sure the framing represents the work accurately. If it is useful to your audience, you are welcome to share it.

Patrick

## Practitioner template

**Subject:** A practical five-signal documentation audit

Hi [name],

I published a practical guide to auditing technical documentation across ownership, discovery, accuracy, launch alignment, and feedback.

I thought it might be relevant to your work on [specific topic]:
https://techdocstudio.com/guides/technical-documentation-audit.html

If you see a missing signal or useful counterexample, I would value the feedback.

Patrick

## Tracking

For each message, record the target, specific relevance, article, send date, reply, share or link, and any editorial correction. Do not send a follow-up unless the recipient engaged or the new message adds substantive value.
