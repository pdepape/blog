## First edition

**Title:** Five signals that reveal whether technical documentation is healthy

Technical documentation problems rarely begin with bad sentences. They begin with missing ownership, weak discovery, untested instructions, disconnected launches, and feedback that never reaches a backlog.

I use five signals to start a documentation audit:

1. **Ownership:** Critical product areas have a named documentation owner and review trigger.
2. **Discovery:** Readers can find the right page using their own language.
3. **Accuracy:** Important instructions and examples are tested against the current product.
4. **Launch alignment:** Documentation changes ship with the product change they explain.
5. **Feedback:** Search failures and support questions become prioritized documentation work.

This is deliberately smaller than a full content inventory. It gives a team enough evidence to agree on the most important operational weakness and create a 30-day improvement plan.
